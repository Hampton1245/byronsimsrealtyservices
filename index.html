Meet Byron Sims

Experience, integrity, and a commitment to service.

Based in Irving, Texas, Byron Sims is a REALTOR® affiliated with eXp Realty LLC, serving clients across the Dallas–Fort Worth metroplex since 2016. His experience spans residential and commercial real estate, helping buyers, sellers, and investors navigate important property decisions with confidence.

His professional background includes high-value real estate transactions approaching and exceeding $1 million. From first-time home purchases to investment opportunities, commercial properties, and complex transactions, Byron brings a detail-oriented perspective and a commitment to thoughtful client guidance.

Alongside his real estate career, Byron has experience in paralegal work and notarial services. His broader professional background, including service as an ordained minister, reflects a people-centered approach grounded in trust, professionalism, and meaningful relationships.
